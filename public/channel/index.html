Channel P&L

Revenue from FinCore by deal source — cash collected or ARR booked, set by the Revenue dropdown above; the other stays on the table as a memo and the costs never change with it. Channel spend is each channel's own money, straight from the marketing sheet and the ledger — nothing touches it. Every other ledger cost appears here only if it is switched on in the Allocation Studio, on the line and by the rule you set there. COGS and Admin stay separate assumptions on the Assumptions tab — set a rate there and they appear here, giving you CM1 and Net.

Allocation Studio

Every expense account in the ledger, and whether it belongs in the channel P&L at all. Switch one on, choose the line it sits on and the rule that spreads it across the channels — nothing is included until you say so. The scope decides who shares the cost and every channel in it gets a share: a channel with no spend is not skipped, it takes its revenue-proportional slice while the channels that did spend share the rest. Pick one group and the whole amount stays inside it; pick Choose groups / channels… and tick as many groups as you like — Marketing Led + Partnership Led, say — and the whole amount is shared across those. Channel spend is never touched here: ad spend comes from the consolidated marketing sheet and partner and referral payments come straight from the ledger, so the accounts behind that row are locked. COGS and Admin stay separate assumptions on the next tab. Rules are held per calendar year and shared with the team.

Assumptions

COGS and Admin are not tracked by channel in the books, so on the P&L they are applied as a percentage of each channel's revenue — cash collected or ARR booked, whichever basis the Revenue dropdown is set to. Enter a rate and the line appears — COGS brings CM1 with it, Admin brings Net. The switches below turn a line back off if you want to see the P&L without it. Rates are held per year. Every ledger cost is handled separately, on the Allocation Studio tab. Settings are shared with the team.
